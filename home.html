    What we actually verify

    The first global search platform dedicated to checking clinics against official sources — not marketing claims.

    • Government-recognized medical accreditation

      Certificates issued by national health authorities, not self-declared badges.

    • Valid medical tourism licenses

      Permits that actually allow a facility to treat patients from abroad.

    • Regulatory compliance

      Published inspection findings from official care inspectorates.

    • Applicable insurance coverage

      Whether your policy is likely to be accepted at the facility.
